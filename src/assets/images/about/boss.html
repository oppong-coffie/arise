How it started

The Story of Arise

Arise was born from a realization—no matter how brilliant the innovations engineers build, their impact is limited if the market isn’t ready for them. The founder, driven by a mission to close the gap between innovation and adoption, started Arise to tackle a core challenge: educating the market.

It began with a simple insight—Africa’s tech potential wasn’t being met not because of a lack of talent, but because of a lack of awareness, understanding, and readiness among consumers. People didn’t know what to do with the technology being created around them.

Arise launched as a media and education initiative focused on tech awareness, literacy, and adoption. The goal was clear: make technology understandable, relevant, and accessible to the average African consumer. Through shows like A Day in Tech, a vibrant YouTube channel, workshops, and community events, Arise grew into a bridge between innovators and the public.

Today, Arise is more than a company—it’s a movement preparing the African market to meet innovation with understanding, confidence, and action.

Kane Mani, Ceo

Mission

Empowerment through Education

At Arise, we are committed to transforming Africa’s technological landscape by prioritizing education. We understand that for technology to truly impact lives, people must first understand it. This value reflects our dedication to making technology accessible, understandable, and relevant to everyone, regardless of their background or experience. We equip individuals with the skills and knowledge they need to navigate and thrive in the digital world.

Innovation with Purpose

We don’t just create for the sake of creating. Every product, service, and initiative we undertake is driven by a deep sense of purpose: to solve real-world problems and improve lives. Arise leverages the power of innovation to meet the unique challenges faced by African consumers, always ensuring that our solutions are not only cutting-edge but also meaningful and impactful to the communities we serve.

Community-Centered Growth

We believe that the true power of technology lies in its ability to connect people, foster collaboration, and enhance communities. Our focus is on building and sustaining meaningful relationships—whether with users, partners, or local stakeholders. By aligning ourselves with the needs of our communities, we ensure that our growth is both sustainable and inclusive. As we expand, we do so with the vision of lifting those around us, creating mutual value for all.
